Daily Jar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026. Daily Jar ("the app") is a simple savings notebook made by Augrixs.

What the app collects

The app itself collects nothing. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. The deposits, withdrawals, notes and settings you type are saved only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. We do not receive, upload, sell or share this data, and we cannot see it.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. When you choose "Back up" or "Export CSV" in the menu, the file is created on your device by your own action, and when you choose "Restore" you pick a file yourself.

Not a bank

Daily Jar does not hold, move or process real money. It only records numbers that you type, so it is not a financial service.

Deleting your data

All data is on your device. To delete it, use Menu, then "Delete everything", or uninstall the app. There is no account to delete.

Children

PIN

If you set a PIN, only a scrambled (hashed) version is stored on your device. It is never sent anywhere and cannot be recovered. If you forget it, the only way back in is to erase the app's data.

Ads

The Android app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show ads and measure them, according to Google's Privacy Policy. Where required, the app asks for your consent first. You can reset or turn off personalised ads in your phone's Google settings. The web app shows no ads. Your deposits, withdrawals and notes are never sent to us or to advertisers.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Augrixs/Daily-Jar/issues.
